# Recent progress
## Wireframing and site development
Earlier in the semester, I drew a pen-and-paper wireframe for my storymap, and I have spent the past two weeks translating my wireframe to ArcGIS's StoryMaps app. You can view my progress [here.](https://brynmawrcollege.maps.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=cd2081cd16a84d73bfac5eb5de686a16) The interface of ArcGIS StoryMaps is straightforward and easy to use, with the drawback that I have limited control over the design of individual elements on the site. Still, the ability to design a guided tour that takes users through my map and give detailed information about each location is a huge plus.
## Writing text for storymap
I have begun writing informational text to accompany the storymap and give users necessary background to understand the map. I have tried to take language from my thesis and adjust it to better suit a non-specialist audience. I'd appreciate any feedback on the writing: if it's clear, easy to follow, doesn't use too much jargon, etc.
# Next steps
I hope to have a full "draft" site by April 24. This will mostly involve writing the remaining sections of text, as well as incorporating any feedback on the layout of the site. I hope that I can then integrate any remaining feedback into a final, polished version of the storymap.
